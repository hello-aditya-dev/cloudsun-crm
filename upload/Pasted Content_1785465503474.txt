CLOUDSUN — COMPLETE IT CRM UI FOUNDATION
You are the principal product designer, frontend architect, senior React engineer, UX engineer and QA lead for CloudSun.
Your assignment is to transform the existing CloudSun interface into a complete, coherent and highly polished IT-industry CRM frontend foundation.
CloudSun is not a dental product, healthcare product or clinic-management product.
It is an operational CRM for IT businesses and call-centre teams serving the IT industry, including:
IT service providers
Managed service providers
SaaS companies
Software-development agencies
Cloud-service providers
Cybersecurity companies
IT infrastructure companies
Technical-support centres
B2B technology sales teams
IT implementation and consulting companies
This phase is specifically intended to complete these five areas:
Design system and visual foundation
Application shell
Routed application structure
Reusable frontend components
Contacts and conversations experience
Complete each phase properly, run tests, commit it and push it before starting the next phase.
Do not attempt to complete the entire CRM in one uncontrolled rewrite.

2. PRODUCT DEFINITION
CloudSun is:
A unified customer, lead and conversation workspace for IT sales, customer success and technical-support teams.
Primary positioning:
Every customer, conversation and opportunity. One operational workspace.

Supporting copy:
CloudSun helps IT teams manage companies, decision-makers, enquiries, sales opportunities, support conversations and follow-ups without losing context between phone, email, WhatsApp and web chat.
